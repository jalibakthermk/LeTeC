LED

Light efficiency is the share of electrical power that leaves as light (25–40 % for white LEDs). If unsure, use 30 %.

Cooling

How it is calculated

Heat Q = N × I × Vf × (1 − efficiency)

Tj = Ta + Q × Rcooling + (Q / N) × Rth,j-sp

Still air: the board loses heat by natural convection and radiation. The heat transfer coefficient is worked out from the board's temperature rise and size, and is typically 8–15 W/m²K. A white solder mask is assumed (emissivity 0.9). The result is about ±20 %, and less accurate inside a closed housing, which runs hotter.

Heat sink: uses the heat sink's thermal resistance from its datasheet. Include the board and thermal pad in it if they are significant.
